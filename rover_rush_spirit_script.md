## 5. The Codex (Main Menu Archive)
*Unlocked after completing the level. This is the deep-dive educational content based on real NASA data.*

**Archive File: MER-A (Spirit)**
*   **Mission Profile:** NASA’s Spirit rover was one half of an identical twin robot pair (alongside Opportunity) sent to rewrite our understanding of the early history of Mars. It landed on January 3, 2004, at Gusev Crater, a possible former lake inside a giant impact crater. Its landing site was named the Columbia Memorial Station.
*   **The Journey:** Spirit's early traverse included traveling 250 meters northeast to a large 200-meter-wide crater, before heading toward the east hills located about 3 kilometers away from the lander. By the time its mission concluded in 2010, Spirit had journeyed a total of 4.8 miles (7.7 kilometers) across the Martian surface. 
*   **Major Discoveries:** Spirit's primary objective was to look for signs of past water. By studying the Martian rock record, Spirit successfully confirmed that water was long standing on the surface of Mars in ancient times. 
*   **Hardware & Imaging:** Spirit was equipped with a panoramic camera (Pancam) that combined data from green, blue, and infrared filters to create approximate true-color images of the Martian landscape. The rover was powered by solar panels that were visible at the front of its chassis.
