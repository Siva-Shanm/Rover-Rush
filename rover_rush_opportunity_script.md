## 5. The Codex (Main Menu Archive)

*Unlocked after completing the level. This is the deep-dive educational content based on real NASA data.*

**Archive File: MER-B (Opportunity)**

* **Mission Profile:** NASA’s Opportunity rover was the identical twin to Spirit, landing on the opposite side of Mars on January 24, 2004, at Meridiani Planum—a site targeted because its mineral deposits suggested a wet Martian history. Like Spirit, its primary objective was to search for signs of past water.

* **Early Exploration & The "Lion King" Panorama:** Opportunity began its mission inside "Eagle Crater," thoroughly examining the rock outcrop behind its lander and the surrounding soils before successfully exiting toward the large crater dubbed "Endurance." On sols 58 and 60, its panoramic camera (Pancam) captured 558 images (over 75 megabytes of data) using infrared, green, and blue filters to form the famous approximate true-color "Lion King" panorama.

* **Endurance & Distance Record:** Aided by Martian winds that periodically cleaned dust off its solar panels (documented in a March 2014 self-portrait), Opportunity far outlived its original mission. By the time it fell silent, Opportunity had accrued 28.06 miles (45.16 kilometers) of driving—holding the off-Earth roving distance record for over eight years until NASA's Perseverance rover passed the mark on October 11, 2026.

* **Major Discoveries & Final Transmission:** By studying the Martian rock record, Opportunity and Spirit confirmed that liquid water was long standing on the surface of Mars in ancient times. Opportunity last communicated with Earth on June 10, 2018, when a planet-wide dust storm blanketed its solar panels and cut off its power.
