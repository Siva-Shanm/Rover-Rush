## 5. The Codex (Main Menu Archive)
*Unlocked after completing the level. This is the deep-dive educational content based on real NASA data.*

**Archive File: Mars Pathfinder & Sojourner (The Pioneers)**
*   **Mission Profile:** Launched on December 4, 1996, Mars Pathfinder and its rover, Sojourner, landed on July 4, 1997, in Ares Vallis—a rocky, ancient flood plain in Mars' northern hemisphere. The resting spot was named the Carl Sagan Memorial Station.
*   **The Landing & Hardware:** Pathfinder pioneered an innovative airbag landing system that bounced about 15 times—reaching up to 50 feet high—and rolled roughly 1 kilometer from its initial impact point before coming to a stop. Sojourner, standing just 1 foot tall and weighing 23 pounds, deployed down a 20-degree ramp to explore the surface.
*   **Major Discoveries:** Sojourner's analysis of rounded pebbles suggested that flowing liquid water existed during a warmer period in Mars' past. The mission also discovered that airborne Martian dust has magnetic properties and observed early-morning clouds composed of water ice.
*   **Mission Legacy:** Before its final transmission on September 27, 1997, the Pathfinder mission returned 2.3 billion bits of information, proving that low-cost mobile exploration on the Red Planet was possible.
