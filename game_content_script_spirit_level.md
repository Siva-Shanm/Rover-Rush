## 5. The Codex (Main Menu Archive)
*Unlocked after completing the level. This is the deep-dive educational content based on real NASA data.*

**Archive File: MER-A (Spirit)**
*   **Mission Profile:** Spirit (Mars Exploration Rover - A) was launched by NASA on June 10, 2003, and landed in Gusev Crater on January 3, 2004. Nicknamed the "robotic geologist," its primary mission was to study rocks, soil, and the environment to search for evidence of ancient water.
*   **Engineering Marvel:** Designed for a 90-Martian-day mission, Spirit operated for over 6 years—more than 25 times its planned duration! It traveled a total distance of approximately 7.73 km, powered entirely by solar panels and rechargeable batteries.
*   **Major Discoveries:** Spirit revolutionized our understanding of Mars. It found rocks altered by water, proving Mars was once much wetter. In 2007, a broken wheel inadvertently dug a trench that revealed almost pure silica, suggesting the past presence of ancient hot springs or steam vents. It also discovered sulfur-rich soil and captured groundbreaking photographs of dust devils.
*   **Final Rest:** In 2009, Spirit became trapped in soft soil (an area named Troy). Unable to maneuver its solar panels toward the sun for the harsh Martian winter, it eventually stopped communicating with Earth in March 2010. The mission officially concluded on May 25, 2011, leaving behind a legacy that proved Mars once had conditions capable of supporting microbial life.
